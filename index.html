Thirty months

Rules

  1. A 12-hour week is a successful week. Not a shortfall.
  2. Above 20 hours, stop. A burned week costs you the next one.
  3. No new domains. The plan is finished. Nothing left to design.
  4. "Shipped" means it exists and someone could look at it.
  5. Hours are honest or this is worthless. Nobody can check them but you.
  6. Missed weeks get logged, not erased. No catching up, no restarting.
  7. Three consecutive missed weeks → stop and diagnose, don't continue.
  8. OMSCS application in by month 4. It's the structure everything leans on.
  9. One human contact point by month 3.
  10. Never copy-paste code from a video. Type it. The typing is the learning.

What not to use

  1. Any agentic AI content from 2023–24. Predates MCP and context engineering entirely.
  2. Udemy and DataCamp AI bundles. Breadth without depth.
  3. Kaggle as a career strategy. Correlates weakly with all of this.
  4. Roadmaps listing thirty tools. Written for search traffic.
  5. Fine-tuning tutorials before Stage B is done. You can't diagnose results you don't understand.
  6. Studying maths before starting the videos. Learn each piece when the code needs it.
